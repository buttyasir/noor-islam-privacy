Privacy Policy — Noor Islam

Effective date: 20 October 2026
Developer: PlatePixels (Yasir Mahmood), Avenue des Démineurs 21, 1090 Brussels, Belgium
Contact: admin@platepixels.com

Noor Islam ("the App") provides prayer times, Qibla direction, an Islamic calendar, a Quran and Hadith library, worship tools and Islamic baby names. This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is handled.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account or registration.
  • The App does not sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • The App does not contain advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your preferences and progress are stored only on your device.

2. Information the App uses

2.1 Location (optional)

If you grant location permission, the App uses your device's approximate or precise location to calculate prayer times, the Qibla bearing and the distance to Makkah, to show your city name, to compute the position of the Sun for the Qibla finder, and to find nearby mosques and halal places.

  • Your coordinates are processed on your device.
  • When you use the "Nearby Mosques & Halal" feature, your current coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de) to search for nearby places. No name, account or device identifier is sent with the request.
  • Your city name may be resolved with your device's operating-system geocoding service.
  • You can deny location permission and instead choose your city manually.

2.2 Notifications (optional)

If you grant notification permission, the App schedules local notifications for prayer times and Adhan. Notifications are generated and scheduled on your device.

2.3 Data stored on your device

The App saves the following in local storage only: language and theme, prayer calculation method and Fiqh school, Adhan settings, your saved location, favourite baby names, Tasbih and Adhkar counters, worship, fasting and Quran-reading tracker progress, and Zakat calculator inputs. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

2.4 Audio and text-to-speech

Adhan and library audio are played from files bundled with the App. Arabic pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

2.5 Sensors

The App reads your device's compass or motion sensors, where available, to orient the Qibla compass. Sensor data is used in real time on your device and is not stored or transmitted.

3. Data sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except that the Overpass API request described in section 2.1 is transmitted to that service to return nearby places. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies to that request.

4. Data retention and deletion

We do not collect or hold your data on our servers. To delete all locally stored data, clear the App's storage in your device settings or uninstall the App.

5. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Security

Because data is stored on your device, it is protected by your device's security. Requests to the Overpass API use HTTPS.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions or privacy requests (including under the GDPR), contact admin@platepixels.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
